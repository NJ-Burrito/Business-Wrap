Brand v3: Cover and Rule

The tool dresses like the documents it makes: paper ground, a navy spine with a gold cap on the start side, chapter headings in navy, and one gold rule that marks what matters. Every colour is a step of the documents' own palette; nothing is retuned.

MOVE 1

The Spine

A navy band on the inline-start edge with a gold cap at its top. It grows top to bottom when a page or panel opens, and it changes sides with the language (English left, Arabic right).

MOVE 2

The Rule

A short gold rule that draws under one phrase from its reading start. Once per view. It marks words; it never fills behind them, so text is readable from the first frame.

02Logo

The mark is a document cover: sheet, spine, gold cap, two ink lines (the second ends at the end: the other edition reads the other way) and The Rule. It is never mirrored for Arabic; only the lockup order changes. Clear space: the spine width on every side. Minimum: mark 16 px, lockup 24 px high.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the files as they are; dark files on #001e3d or darker.
  • Keep gold for one mark per view: The Rule or one gold fill.
  • Pair every coloured status with a word and an icon.

Don't

  • Set words in gold on light grounds.
  • Mirror the mark for Arabic, stretch it or add effects.
  • Change a text colour during an animation.
